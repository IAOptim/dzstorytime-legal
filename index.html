DzStoryTime

Automated storytelling about Algerian history, published to TikTok.

What DzStoryTime does

DzStoryTime is an automated content tool that produces and publishes short-form educational videos about the history of Algeria to the DzStoryTime TikTok account. Each episode is written from verified historical sources, narrated, illustrated, and assembled into a vertical video, then published to TikTok.

How it works with TikTok

  • Login Kit is used to securely authenticate and connect the DzStoryTime TikTok account (scope user.info.basic).
  • Content Posting API (scope video.upload) is used to upload the generated short-form videos to the connected TikTok account.
  • Every video is reviewed and validated by a human before it goes live. Nothing is posted without approval.

Content & sources

All content is educational and based on published historical works. Sources are credited on screen and in each post's description. DzStoryTime does not publish sponsored, misleading, or automated spam content.
